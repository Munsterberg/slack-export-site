How to export Slack messages without admin access

Slack's built-in export is only for workspace owners and admins. Channel Export works for regular members. It saves the channels, DMs, and threads you can already see to a file on your computer.

Add to Chrome, free For Slack in Chrome, at app.slack.com

Steps

  1. Add Channel Export to Chrome.
  2. Open Slack at app.slack.com in Chrome and sign in to your workspace.
  3. Open the channel, DM, or thread you want and click Export in the header.
  4. Pick a format and a date range. Markdown and CSV are free. HTML and PDF are in the $7 Full export. The file saves to your Downloads folder.

Nothing gets installed in your workspace, and nobody has to approve a Slack app. The extension uses your own Slack sign-in, so it only reaches conversations your account can already open. It reads them and never posts, edits, or deletes anything.

The whole export runs in your browser. We have no server that receives your messages. The privacy policy has the details.

The other options are asking an owner to run Slack's export, copying and pasting from the web app (fine for a short chat, slow for a long one because Slack loads history as you scroll), or a command-line tool such as slackdump.

Markdown and CSV are free, with no message limits and no account. Full export is a single $7 payment, not a subscription. It adds HTML with images, files, avatars, and custom emoji in a ZIP that works offline, PDF through Chrome's Save as PDF, files and images downloaded into the export, and exporting several conversations at once.

This extension is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Slack Technologies, LLC. Your workspace may still have rules about keeping copies of messages, especially at work. Check them before you export.

Questions

Do I need to be a Slack admin to export messages?

No. Slack's built-in export is only for workspace owners and admins. Channel Export works for regular members. If you can open a conversation in Slack, you can export it.

Will my workspace admin be notified?

The extension doesn't install anything in your workspace or post anything. It asks Slack for messages through your own browser session, the same way the Slack web app does. Your workspace may still have rules about keeping copies, so check them first.

Is it free?

Markdown and CSV are free, with no message limits and no account. Full export adds HTML, PDF, files and images, and exporting several conversations at once. It's a single $7 payment, not a subscription.
