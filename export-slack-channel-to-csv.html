How to export a Slack channel to CSV

A CSV export gives you one row per message, ready to open in Excel or Google Sheets. Channel Export makes one from any channel, private channel, DM, or thread you can see. CSV is free, with no message limits.

Add to Chrome, free For Slack in Chrome, at app.slack.com

Steps

  1. Add Channel Export to Chrome.
  2. Open the channel at app.slack.com, or pick it from the extension's toolbar popup.
  3. Click Export and choose CSV.
  4. Pick a date range and whether to include thread replies, then save.

Use it to count requests, review a support channel, track decisions, or hand a record to someone who doesn't use Slack.

The export runs in your browser using your own Slack sign-in, so it only covers conversations your account can already open. It does not need workspace admin rights. We have no server that receives your messages. The privacy policy has the details.

Markdown is free as well, if you want a document instead of a spreadsheet. HTML, PDF through Chrome's Save as PDF, files and images downloaded into the export, and exporting several conversations at once are in Full export, a single $7 payment.

This extension is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Slack Technologies, LLC. Your workspace may still have rules about keeping copies of messages, especially at work. Check them before you export.

Questions

Can I export a private channel to CSV?

Yes, if you're a member of it. If you can open the channel in Slack, you can export it.

Is there a message limit?

No. Markdown and CSV are free, with no message limits and no account. You still get only the history Slack shows you. On free Slack plans, Slack only shows the last 90 days, so older messages can't be reached.

Can I pick a date range?

Yes. Export all time, the last 30 days, or your own start and end dates.
