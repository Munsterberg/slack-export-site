Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version

  • Your Slack messages, files, and sign-in token stay in your browser. We have no server that receives them.
  • The extension only reads from Slack. It can't post, edit, or delete anything.
  • We collect a few anonymous usage events, and you can turn them off.

What the extension does

Channel Export is a Chrome extension that exports Slack conversations you already have access to (channels, direct messages, group messages, and threads) into files on your computer. It works with the Slack session you are already signed in to at app.slack.com.

Your Slack data

When you start an export, the extension requests messages, replies, user profiles, and custom emoji from Slack's API, directly from your browser to Slack. It builds the export file in your browser and saves it with Chrome's download feature.

  • Message content, file contents, channel names, people's names, and workspace details are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • The extension uses read-only Slack API methods. It contains no code that posts, edits, reacts to, or deletes anything in Slack.
  • Exported files are saved only to your computer. We don't keep a copy.

Your Slack sign-in token

To make requests on your behalf, the extension reads the session token from the Slack page you have open. The token never leaves your browser and is only sent to Slack itself. It is kept in Chrome's session storage while an export runs, so an interrupted export can resume, and Chrome clears it when you close the browser. It is never written to long-term storage.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn whether exports work and which features people use, the extension sends anonymous events to PostHog, our analytics provider:

  • install: the extension was installed
  • export_started, export_completed: an export began or finished
  • export_failed: an export failed, with a general reason such as "rate limited" or "signed out"
  • The export format, and the size of the export in broad buckets (for example "1,000 to 10,000 messages")
  • paid_unlock: the paid version was unlocked

These events never contain message content, file names, channel or conversation names, people's names, Slack user, channel, or workspace IDs, or workspace domains. Like any web request, they reach PostHog with your IP address. PostHog is configured to discard it rather than store it. You can turn analytics off at any time under Settings in the extension popup, and no further events are sent.

Payments

The paid version is a one-time purchase processed by ExtensionPay using Stripe. When you buy, ExtensionPay and Stripe receive the details you enter at checkout, such as your email address and card, under their own privacy policies. We never see your full card details. The extension stores only whether you have paid, in Chrome's local storage, so it doesn't need to check every time. The free formats never check payment status.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • Access to slack.com and slack-edge.com: to add the Export button and to fetch messages, files, avatars, and emoji from Slack. The extension can't read any other site.
  • Downloads: to save the export file to your computer.
  • Storage: to remember your settings, the paid status, and the progress of a running export.
  • Offscreen document: to build PDFs and ZIP files in the background without opening a tab.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Not affiliated with Slack

This extension is an independent project. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Slack Technologies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change would send any new kind of data off your device, we'll ask for your consent in the extension first.
