Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version

  • Your Slack messages, files, and sign-in token stay in your browser. We have no server that receives them.
  • The extension only reads from Slack. It never posts, edits, or deletes anything.
  • We collect a few anonymous usage events, and you can turn them off.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

Channel Export is made by OPERATOR_NAME. In this policy, "we" means OPERATOR_NAME. Channel Export is a Chrome extension that exports Slack conversations you already have access to (channels, direct messages, group DMs, and threads) into files on your computer. It works with the Slack session you are already signed in to at app.slack.com.

Your Slack data

When you start an export, the extension requests messages, replies, channel details, user profiles, and custom emoji from Slack's API, directly from your browser to Slack. For a Full export, it also downloads files, images, and avatars from Slack's file servers. It builds the export file in your browser and saves it with Chrome's download feature.

  • Message content, file contents, channel names, people's names, and workspace details are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • The extension uses read-only Slack API methods. It contains no code that posts, edits, reacts to, or deletes anything in Slack.
  • Exported files are saved only to your computer. We don't keep a copy.

Your Slack sign-in token

To make requests on your behalf, the extension reads your session token from the Slack page you have open. Once you open Slack in Chrome, the extension keeps the token in Chrome's session storage, which is held in memory, until you close Chrome or the extension updates. It is never written to disk, and it is only ever sent to Slack.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn whether exports work and which features people use, the extension sends anonymous events to PostHog, our analytics provider:

  • install: the extension was installed
  • export_started, export_completed: an export began or finished
  • export_failed: an export failed, with a general reason such as "rate limited" or "signed out"
  • The export format, and the size of the export in broad buckets (for example "1,000 to 10,000 messages")
  • paid_unlock: Full export was unlocked

Each event also carries a random ID created when the extension is installed, the extension version, and your browser and operating system versions. The random ID isn't linked to you, your email, or your Slack account. Events never contain message content, file names, channel or conversation names, people's names, Slack user, channel, or workspace IDs, or workspace domains. Like any web request, they reach PostHog with your IP address. PostHog is configured to discard it rather than store it.

The install event is sent once, right after installation, before you have a chance to change settings. After that, you can turn analytics off at any time under Settings in the extension popup, and no further events are sent. We keep analytics events for up to one year. Because they aren't linked to you, we can't find or delete a particular person's events.

Payments

Full export is a one-time purchase processed by ExtensionPay using Stripe. When you buy, ExtensionPay and Stripe receive the details you enter at checkout, such as your email address and card, under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details.

To check whether you've paid, the extension sends ExtensionPay a random ID created for your installation. ExtensionPay's code stores that ID and your payment status in Chrome's synced storage, so it follows your Chrome profile. The extension only checks payment for Full export features. Markdown and CSV exports never check payment status.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • Access to slack.com and slack-edge.com: to add the Export button and to fetch messages, files, avatars, and emoji from Slack.
  • Access to extensionpay.com: so ExtensionPay's checkout page can tell the extension when a payment completes. This is the only other site the extension can access.
  • Downloads: to save the export file to your computer.
  • Storage: to remember your settings, your payment status, and export progress, and to hold your Slack session token in memory-only session storage.
  • Offscreen document: to run exports in the background without opening a tab.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

On your device only, the extension handles authentication information (your Slack session token), personal communications (the messages you export), and personal information about the people in them (names and avatars). Our use of information received from Slack and Chrome adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.
