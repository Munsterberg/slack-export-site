How to export a Slack thread

Sometimes you only need one conversation: a decision, an incident, or a client request. Channel Export saves a single thread, with each reply placed under the message it answers.

Add to Chrome, free For Slack in Chrome, at app.slack.com

Steps

  1. Add Channel Export to Chrome.
  2. Open the thread in Slack at app.slack.com.
  3. Click Export in the thread panel.
  4. Pick a format. Markdown and CSV are free. HTML and PDF are in the $7 Full export. The file saves to your Downloads folder.

Markdown is the easiest way to move a thread into a doc, a ticket, or a notes app. CSV keeps timestamps and authors in columns if you want to sort or filter.

You can also export a whole channel and choose whether to include thread replies. In a free export, files are linked back to Slack. Full export downloads the files and images into the export, saves the thread as HTML or as a PDF through Chrome's Save as PDF, and can export several conversations at once.

The export runs in your browser and only includes what your Slack account can already see. We have no server that receives your messages. The privacy policy has the details. This extension is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Slack Technologies, LLC.

Your workspace may still have rules about keeping copies of messages, especially at work. Check them before you export.

Questions

Can I export a thread to PDF?

Yes, with Full export. Choose PDF in the export form and the extension opens the conversation in a tab for Chrome's print dialog. Pick Save as PDF there. Markdown and CSV stay free, with no limits.

Are files and images included?

In a free Markdown or CSV export, files are linked back to Slack. Full export downloads the files and images into the export. HTML also includes images, files, avatars, and custom emoji, in a ZIP that works offline.

Is it free?

Markdown and CSV are free, with no message limits and no account. Full export adds HTML, PDF, files and images, and exporting several conversations at once. It's a single $7 payment, not a subscription.
